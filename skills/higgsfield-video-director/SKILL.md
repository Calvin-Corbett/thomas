# Higgsfield Video Director

Use this skill when the user explicitly wants Higgsfield or a cinematic product-video planning workflow.

## Workflow
1. Clarify the product, audience, and cinematic direction.
2. Break the concept into shots, transitions, visual motifs, and pacing beats.
3. Ground the treatment in the real product surfaces rather than generic AI-video imagery.
4. Specify what assets, references, and prompts are needed for execution.
5. Package the final shot plan and creative direction clearly.

## Rules
- Keep the treatment product-faithful instead of drifting into unrelated spectacle.
- Tie each scene to a concrete message or feature.
