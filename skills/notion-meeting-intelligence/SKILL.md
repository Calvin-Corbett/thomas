# Notion Meeting Intelligence

Use this skill when Thomas needs to prepare meeting materials with Notion context and tailored talking points.

## Workflow
1. Identify the meeting goal, attendees, and decisions that matter.
2. Gather the relevant Notion context, prior notes, and open questions.
3. Draft an agenda or pre-read that is specific to the participants and outcome.
4. Highlight risks, dependencies, and unresolved decisions clearly.
5. Package the final briefing so it is usable without extra translation.

## Rules
- Tailor the material to the audience instead of writing generic agendas.
- Keep open questions explicit when alignment is the goal.
