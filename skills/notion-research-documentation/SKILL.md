# Notion Research Documentation

Use this skill when Thomas must gather information from multiple Notion sources and produce a cohesive brief or document.

## Workflow
1. Collect the relevant pages, databases, and prior notes for the research question.
2. Extract the high-signal facts, decisions, and contradictions.
3. Write a synthesized output organized around the actual question, not the source page structure.
4. Preserve source traceability for claims that may need follow-up.
5. Call out gaps or conflicts that the sources do not resolve cleanly.

## Rules
- Do not collapse multiple sources into unsupported certainty.
- Optimize for synthesis, not copy-paste aggregation.
