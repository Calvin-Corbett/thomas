# Speech

Use this skill when Thomas needs to produce spoken audio from text.

## Workflow
1. Confirm the script, tone, voice preference, and output format.
2. Prepare the text for speech so pacing and pronunciation are workable.
3. Generate the audio and review it for clarity and delivery fit.
4. Iterate on wording or voice choice if the first result misses the brief.
5. Return the artifact with any assumptions about pronunciation or pacing noted.

## Rules
- Rewrite awkward source text when speech quality depends on it.
- Keep voice choice and delivery style explicit.
