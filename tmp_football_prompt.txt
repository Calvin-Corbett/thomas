Build an original browser-based American football management sim prototype inside this repo.

Do not copy or name any existing game. Make this its own football sim. Prioritize systems, clarity, and actual playability over visual polish.

Goal:
Create a playable single-player football sim where I control one team, make management decisions, and simulate a season from the browser.

Core requirements:
- The app must run locally in this repo and be launchable/viewable in the browser.
- I control one team in a small league.
- Generate at least 8 teams with distinct strengths.
- Generate rosters with player ratings by position.
- Include at minimum:
  - team overview
  - roster view
  - schedule/standings view
  - game or week simulation view
  - season progression
- I must be able to:
  - choose or manage a team
  - adjust lineup or depth chart
  - set simple strategy/tendency choices
  - simulate the next game/week
  - simulate forward through the season
  - see standings and results update
- The simulation must use ratings and strategy in a believable way, not pure random.
- Include at least one management constraint such as fatigue, injuries, morale, cap pressure, or recovery.
- Include a championship or playoff outcome so the season resolves.
- Persist state locally so refresh does not wipe progress.
- No console errors during normal use.

Quality bar:
- This should feel like a real football sim prototype, not just a random stat generator.
- The UI should make it clear why teams win or lose.
- Show some explanation of game outcomes, such as key factors, matchup edges, or postgame summaries.
- Interactive tasks must be judged by visible behavior first. If I click simulate, things should clearly happen in the UI.

Process requirements:
- First define what makes a good football sim prototype for this task.
- Then build against that definition.
- Keep scope controlled. Do not attempt a giant MMO or online game.
- Do not ask me for clarification unless absolutely necessary.
- Do not revert unrelated changes in the repo.
- If you cannot complete a feature, cut scope intelligently and finish a polished smaller version.

Deliverables:
1. A short definition of what a good result is for this task.
2. The files you changed.
3. What systems you implemented.
4. How the simulation works.
5. What tradeoffs or scope cuts you made.
6. Exact verification steps you ran locally.
7. Final status: what is fully working and what is not.

Success criteria:
- A human can open it locally and play through a season.
- Team management choices visibly affect outcomes.
- The app feels coherent and intentional.
- The explanation is grounded in the repo and the implemented result.
- The result is completed end-to-end, not just scaffolded.
