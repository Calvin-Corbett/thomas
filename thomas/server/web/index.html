Model Setup

Roles inherit the base model until a specialty override is selected.

The model's own thinking depth (its native setting). Higher spends more time reasoning before it answers — better on hard problems, slower and pricier on easy ones.

How far Thomas goes on its own. L1 Chat — answers only. L2 Assist — drafts and suggests, you approve. L3 Agent — runs multi-step tasks, checks in on big calls. L4 Full — plans and executes end-to-end without stopping to ask.

How hard Thomas works on a task. Brisk — one fast pass, lowest cost. Diligent — a balanced, careful pass. Exhaustive — staffs a crew, runs adversarial review passes and a quality rubric, and self-corrects until it's right (slowest, most expensive).

Open — minimal friction, fewest checks. Guarded — sensible defaults. Fortress — maximum strictness. Safety-critical Vault checks (secrets, destructive actions) always stay on and can't be turned off.
