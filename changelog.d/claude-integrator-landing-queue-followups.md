### Fixed

- **A refusal tells you enough to fix it.** When the commit tool refuses your landing, in the slot or in the pre-check, its whole output is kept in `output/T<ticket>-<stage>.txt` under the queue root and land.py prints that path; you see at least 2,000 characters of the gate's own output (it used to be about 300, cut mid-line).
- **`--submit` returns once your ticket exists.** The pre-check dry run moved into the process that waits for your ticket: it runs only with at most 2 tickets ahead, only when one of the 2 queue-wide slots is free (it never waits for one and never runs without one), and it is stopped when your turn comes. A pre-check refusal ends your ticket before it uses the slot and does not count toward the refusal cap.
- **Running the same command again after its worker died lands.** land.py now reaps before it looks for your open ticket and calls it attached only when it gets that very ticket, so the re-run queues again with a new worker and says the old ticket was taken back, instead of waiting on a ticket nobody holds and exiting 4.
- **A worker that dies says why.** Each detached worker writes what it does, and any crash, to `logs/T<ticket>.log`, and an exit 4 names that log. A git call that does not answer in time is read as no answer (not landed) instead of a traceback that killed the worker unseen.
- **Two promises are now pinned by tests:** the fallback tree kill when the OS refuses a job object, and the eight-lease wall bound on a holder whose own run-time count never moves. Pruning a finished ticket now also removes its output files and worker log.
