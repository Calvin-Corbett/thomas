### Changed

- At most two second looks run at once on this computer, counting both of Thomas's servers (`THOMAS_SECOND_LOOKS` sets another number, and both servers need the same value); the others wait in the order they asked. When your Claude plan's usage limit is reached, every second look on either server waits, every held change says when second looks resume, and you get one notice in the issue log for each limit, however long it lasts, with no new one until a second look has succeeded again, instead of each held change asking again every 15 minutes and using up its tries. Second looks resume at the reset time the CLI printed; when that time cannot be read exactly, Thomas looks again at the time it worked out, an hour later when the zone cannot be read, and 30 minutes later when no time was given or the time printed has already passed. A short throttle that is not your plan's limit is no longer reported as one.
