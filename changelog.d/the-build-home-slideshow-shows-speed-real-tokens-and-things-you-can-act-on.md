### Changed

- **The Build home has a "How fast Thomas is going" slide.** You said the slideshow should measure improvement. It shows your average build time, average check time, the time to Thomas's first reply, and tokens a second, with a line saying whether the last five builds were faster or slower than the five before them. Build and check times come from the timings every task already carries. First reply and tokens a second are recorded from the next task you run, and say "not yet" until then instead of showing a zero.
- **Tokens are real.** Each Build run now records the tokens its lead used and how long it took (in the run's own folder), and a new read-only address, `/api/evolve/agent/performance`, totals them by day. The token slide draws a bar per day with its number above it, the total, the last three days, prompt against reply, and reply tokens a second. Until a run has recorded something it says so instead of drawing an empty graph. Thomas needs a restart for runs to start recording.
- **"Tasks worked on" has numbers.** Every bar has its count above it and hover names the day, and a line gives the week's total and the last three days.
- **"What Thomas has noticed" is a set of cards you can act on.** A task in a repair round, a task that took more than twice your average to build, and tasks that stopped without finishing each get a card with a button ("Find out why", "Find what slowed it", "Review them"). The button puts a specific request in the composer for you to read and send; it never sends by itself. When nothing needs attention, the card says so and offers "Look for what to improve".
- **Recent tasks open when you click them**, and the last slide is now "Where to go next": ideas made from your own tasks (pick up the one still running, check the last thing that landed works, speed up the slowest part, review the last day of changes) instead of generic tiles. With no tasks yet it falls back to the starter ideas.
