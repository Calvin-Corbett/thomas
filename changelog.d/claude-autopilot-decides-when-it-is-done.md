### Added

- **Autopilot decides when a build is done, and then stops.** When a build finishes on Autopilot, Thomas checks it against the bar your effort setting sets (Max: used the way you would use it, nothing you asked for missing, polished) and either answers DONE with the checks that prove it and up to three proposals for what to do next, or fixes what is missing and checks again. Effort decides how far he builds; Autopilot decides that he acts instead of asking.
