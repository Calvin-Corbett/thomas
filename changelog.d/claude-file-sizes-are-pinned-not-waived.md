### Changed

- **Large files are pinned at their size instead of waived until a date.** Seven of the waivers in `docs/monolith_guard_baseline.json` had passed their expiry date without anyone noticing, and the whole-repo size guard failed on three files: the expired waiver for `apps/site/src/app/globals.css`, and `scripts/active_folders.py` and `scripts/breakglass_auth.py`, which are over the hard limit with no entry. Every listed file still over the 800-line limit is now frozen at the size it has today: it may shrink, never grow, and there is no expiry date to keep. A freeze gives no headroom, where several waivers allowed growth up to a ceiling above the file's real size. Entries for files now at or under 800 lines are removed (the normal limits apply to them again), and the two unlisted files over the hard limit are added, frozen. The whole-repo guard reports no violations afterwards, and each new entry has its approval row in `docs/ops/monolith_baseline_approvals.json`.
- **Stylesheets and HTML pages are no longer judged by line count.** `monolith_guard.py` reads `size_limit_exempt_extensions` (now `css` and `html`) from the protected baseline: a stylesheet is one cascade and a page is one document, so cutting either at a line number buys nothing. A frozen entry must set `max_growth_lines` to 0 and give a reason; an ordinary waiver still expires. The exemption is recorded in the approvals registry as well, since the approval gate cannot see it.
