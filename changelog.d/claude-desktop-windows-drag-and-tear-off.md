### Added
- **The Thomas window moves, and you can have as many as you like.** You said you couldn't drag the app around or drag a tab off like in Chrome. Press any empty part of the title bar and drag to move the window (double-click maximizes; a maximized window comes away under your pointer), Ctrl+N or "New window" in the profile menu opens another, and a second launch opens another window instead of just focusing the first.
- **Drag a tab off the strip and it becomes its own window.** Pull a tab up or down off the strip and a window opens under your pointer carrying it; let go over another Thomas window's strip and it joins that window. A website tab keeps its page, scroll and history as it moves; a Chat, Build or Work tab opens again in the new window. Tabs also reorder by dragging along the strip.

### Fixed
- **The window was locked in place.** The title bar's one native drag region was covered by the tab strip, which took nearly all of the bar, so there was almost nothing to grab. The bar now moves the window itself.
