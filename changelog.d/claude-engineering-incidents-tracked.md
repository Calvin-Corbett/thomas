### Fixed

- **Engineering incident records were invisible to everything that reads them.** `plans/thomas/problems/*` is deny-by-default in `.gitignore`, with a hand-maintained allowlist. That rule is right and it is there for a reason — a 2026-05-19 incident leaked private content through that directory, and 44 of the records under it are auto-generated from chat. But the same rule made every *engineering* incident untracked: 91 of 94 records did not survive a clean clone, never reached CI, and could not be seen by the problem-closure gate, which is diff-based. The obligations the Praxis phases built — closure, expiry, `REOPEN DUE` — were running on files git does not track, which means they were machine-local and a fresh clone inherited none of them.

- **Separated by origin instead of by hand.** Agent-authored engineering incidents named `ENG-<slug>` are now re-included by a single glob. The chat generator never uses that prefix, so auto-generated records stay ignored and the security property is unchanged; verified after the change as 11 records visible and 82 still ignored, including all 44 `[WIP]` chat records. The alternative — adding two lines per record forever — is why only three had ever been allowlisted. Records written before the convention existed are re-included by name so the board messages citing their paths stay valid.
