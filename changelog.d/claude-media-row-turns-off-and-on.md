### Changed

- **Create image, Create video and Create music can now be turned off and on while Thomas runs.** Their four `/api/media` routes moved out of the server's fixed list into a row called Creating images, video and music, which ships on, so nothing changes until you turn it off. While it is off those routes answer 404, and asking for one in Chat says it could not start, with an error about JSON and nothing to say why. A server started with the row off never loads the routes' code. Turned off on a running server, the routes go at once and their code stays loaded until the next restart. Off does not wait for anything being made: it is stopped and recorded straight away, and a model already running on your computer goes on to its end with nothing reading the result, because Python cannot stop a thread. Anything new on a local model waits behind it. What you already made stays in your chats and Library, and your choice of model or key stays.
