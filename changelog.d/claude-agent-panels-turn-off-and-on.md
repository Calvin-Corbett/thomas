### Changed

- **The first three sections of the Agent Ops page can now be turned off and on while Thomas runs.** Steer a running agent, Live run telemetry and Worktree progress, with their 14 routes, moved out of the server's fixed list into a row called Agent Ops steering and progress, which ships on, so nothing changes until you turn it off. Nothing in Thomas reports to these sections, so your agents, Build runs and chats carry on as before. While it is off those routes answer 404, and the three sections still say LIVE at their top right. After a reload, Steer a running agent shows a red Could not load fleet sessions, Live run telemetry says offline with unknown in every box, and Worktree progress shows a red could not load progress above empty lists. A page already open shows the same warnings and keeps what it last showed, and its buttons fail. The other three sections of Agent Ops are not affected. Turning it off forgets all that these sections held, because they keep it in memory, and turning it back on clears the warnings by themselves within a few seconds, with no reload. A server started with the row off never loads the three route files. Turned off on a running server, the routes go at once and their code stays loaded until the next restart. The two steering routes that list sessions and steers now ask for your token when Thomas is open to other computers. Before this they answered anyone who could reach them.
