### Added

- **Thomas asks you on a card before a setting changes.** You asked for this:
  "if he's going to change a toggle, it should be announced ... he puts like a
  card ... says, hey, do you want me to do this? It will require a password".
  When one of his settings stands in the way (autonomy, the files he can
  change, internet reach), he shows a Thomas card in the chat. It says what he
  wants to change from and to, what the new level means, and why. You press
  Yes or No. Yes on a raised limit brings up the one Thomas password prompt,
  and the change applies only if Windows accepts your PIN or password. The
  card only asks: the server still enforces every raised limit, whatever
  Thomas or the card says.

### Fixed

- **Thomas no longer says a setting changed when it did not.** Asked to go
  offline, he wrote a free-form preference note and replied "Internet reach is
  now Off". Nothing reads those notes as a setting, so your internet setting
  had not changed. Writing a note whose name looks like one of his settings is
  now refused and points him to the card. The tool's own description now says
  that preference notes do not control his settings.
