### Changed

- **The Marketplace, the pages of your installed apps and the plugin store this Thomas can host can now be turned off and on while Thomas runs.** Their 19 routes moved out of the server's fixed list into a row called Marketplace and installed apps, which ships on, so nothing changes until you turn it off. While it is off those routes answer 404. The Marketplace page says it is unavailable and where to turn it back on, and nothing from the store is listed. You cannot install, remove, turn on or turn off anything from it, except in its Plugged in section, which still answers. The page tries again about every minute and a half, even in a tab you have left, and you can scroll it, type in its search box and click on it as usual. Turned back on, it fills in by itself the next time it tries, or at once if you press Sync Catalog. Apps you installed stay installed and keep what they saved, but their pages will not open: after a reload they leave the sidebar and Library with no message, and a desktop shortcut to one shows 404: Not Found. An app page already open goes on working until you reload it. Other computers cannot reach the plugin store on this Thomas. A server started with the row off never loads the three route files. Turned off on a running server, the routes go at once and their code stays loaded until the next restart, along with the store's rate limit and signing secret.

### Fixed

- **Closing a Marketplace tab, or reloading the window, while the page is waiting for its list no longer freezes the whole window.** A failed ask for the list was followed by another at once, so a page that could not get its list asked about 33 times a second for as long as it stayed open. When the tab closed or the window reloaded, each ask failed at once with nothing to wait for, and the page went on asking inside one turn of the window's thread, so the window stopped for good. A slow first load was enough: closing the tab 4 seconds after opening it did this with the Marketplace on. After any failed ask the page now waits 75 seconds before asking by itself, says what went wrong at the top of the page, and still asks at once when you press Sync Catalog.
