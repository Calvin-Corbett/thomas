### Fixed

- **A team that worked a long time is no longer hidden from the project list.** The list read at most 2 MB of a conversation and skipped the rest with "record is too large", so the teams that had done the most work (their records are tens of MB of transcripts) vanished from `GET /api/self/projects`: 79 of 85 projects were skipped on your running copy. The record is streamed in 64 KiB pieces and only what the list shows is kept, so a 50 MB record is read in under 4 MB of memory and the old byte cap is now a 1 GiB safety valve. A record past that valve is named in the report as a project that could not be read, with its reason, instead of being left out; only real corruption is described as "unreadable".
- **The restart pass reads a big conversation the same way.** It cut nothing before: it parsed the whole file (about three times its size in memory at once), and a `MemoryError` or `RecursionError` from one record was not caught, so one big record could stop every other project from being carried on after a restart. It now streams each record with every transcript cut to its tail (40 MB: 120 MB of memory before, 1.3 MB now), and a record it cannot take is retried and then skipped with its reason like any other unreadable one.
