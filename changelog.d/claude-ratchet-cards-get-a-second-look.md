### Changed

- **The ratchet's cards get the same second look as every other change Thomas makes to himself.** A backlog candidate that needed no repair went straight to your card once its proof passed, and so did a repair carried to a newer dev; only a repair settled in its own copy was read by a second model. Now Claude, able only to read, reads each one against what its own commits say it is for before the card. SAFE: the card says a second model read it. NOT SAFE: it is reported with the findings and never reaches you. When the reader cannot run (your plan's usage limit, a sign-in that ended), the candidate waits for the next run instead of going to you unread, and that run stops there rather than prove the candidates after it for nothing. A repair held this way keeps its team's work for the next run to take up; before, it was reported and the work thrown away. Dev and the change are checked again after the read, since a careful read takes minutes.
