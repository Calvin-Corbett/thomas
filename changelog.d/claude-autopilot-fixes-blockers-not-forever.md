### Changed

- **Autopilot stops when the work is finished, and fixes what stops it instead of going on
  forever.** You said: "autopilot ... is meant to actually fix issues with Thomas that
  prevent him from finish not the actually work continuous forever like if I said post an
  ad to Facebook he can't do that so autopilot knows to go download it all because I
  already gave permission to do so". And when the team says it is done, it is done, like
  ChatGPT. So a finished Autopilot run no longer starts a done-check round: a Thomas
  project is handed in to the check before your card, and an ordinary Build stops, the
  same as below Autopilot. A conversation already in the middle of a done check is still
  judged the way it was.
- **A team that cannot finish says what it is missing.** On Autopilot the team ends its
  answer with `BLOCKED: <what is missing> | NEEDS: <what would unblock it>`. If it is
  something you have already allowed (a tool or package to install, something to
  download, a sign-in that is already saved, a missing piece of Thomas), the next round
  gets it and goes back to finish your goal. If the same thing stops it twice in a row
  and nothing changed, it pauses for you instead of trying a third time. If only you can
  give it (a password, a payment or money, a permission you have not granted, an account
  you must create, or a choice that is yours), the line ends `| OWNER` and the project
  pauses at once, saying exactly what it needs from you. Your reply carries it on.
  Spending money, accepting terms, entering passwords or payment details, and posting
  in public always wait for you.
