### Added

- **Parts of Thomas can now turn off and on while it runs.** One list decides what loads, and your choices are saved on top of it. The companion surface and the branch sweep are the first two. Turning one off lets its running work finish first, and a plugin that is off never loads at all. There is no page for this yet: it is reached at `/api/plugins/thomas` from this machine, and the Plugins page comes next.
- **A broken plugin can no longer stop Thomas starting.** It starts without that plugin and reports why.
