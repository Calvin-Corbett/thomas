### Changed

- **You choose each window's length and its number of landings when you open it, and a window can keep landing while you are away.** `window_control.py open` takes `--minutes` up to 1440 (24 hours; 120 when you choose nothing), `--max-landings N` (25 when you choose nothing) or `--no-landing-cap`, and `--away`. With `--away` a window keeps landing while your PC is locked or you are away, instead of pausing and ending after 15 minutes; it still ends at its time, at its number, with an off switch, on a sign-out or restart, or when the check after a landing fails, and protected and cage files still always get their own card. `--away` is off on every PC until its elevated setup turns it on (`enable_window.ps1 -AwayWindows on`, which writes `window.away` into the pin folder that only an elevated step can write), and a `window.away` anyone but SYSTEM and Administrators could have written is refused. Its card has its own title and says it keeps landing, and the window's status and record say so.
