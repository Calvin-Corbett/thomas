### Added

- **The seam finder reads pages by their script blocks.** Three of the remaining oversized files are HTML. Everything outside `<script>` is counted as template and set aside as not code and not cut here; the scripts are read like a JavaScript file, at whichever brace depth covers more of the code, since a page keeps a few constants at the top and everything real inside one closure. Two reader faults found on chat.html are fixed on the way: a regex full of quote characters and a one-line block comment each hid a closing brace and let one unit swallow the rest of the page. Verdicts on the three pages: chat.html has 363 template lines and 2,800 lines of inline script that pack a part to the limit, a design job; settings.html is a template with no code; system_map.html has no seam that fits.
