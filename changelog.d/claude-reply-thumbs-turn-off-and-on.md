### Changed

- **The thumbs under a reply can now be turned off and on while Thomas runs.** The two `/api/chat/feedback` routes moved out of the server's fixed list into a plugin called Reply thumbs, which ships on, so nothing changes until you turn it off. While it is off, the Good reply and Bad reply buttons under each reply in Chat are still there, but a thumb is not saved and nothing tells you so, and a Chat page you already have open does the same without asking again and again. Thumbs you saved earlier stay in their file and are listed again when it is back on.
