### Changed

- **Workspaces, linked projects and source notes can now be turned off and on while Thomas runs.** Their 35 routes moved into one switch on the Plugins page, called Workspaces, linked projects and source notes, which ships on, so nothing changes until you turn it off. While it is off, Library cannot list or open projects and shows 404: Not Found, the project menu beside your message box in Build says it could not read your library, and Load file in Agent Ops shows a 404. Your linked projects, workspaces and notes are kept and come back when you turn it on. Turning it on no longer makes Thomas wait while the workspaces database opens, and turning it off cancels a project scan still going. A Library page left open with this off used to ask for its list every second without stopping, and now stops after one answer.
