### Added
- A pipeline canary watches Thomas's own check before your card. From 2026-09-19 to 2026-10-27, 22 landings broke Thomas's own machinery, every breaking self-fix passed its own check first, and the quiet breaks ran a median of about 18 hours before anyone noticed. Every 5 minutes and at your sign-in, the scheduled task ThomasPipelineCanary reads dev. When dev has moved and the move changed `thomas/server/precard/` or `thomas/server/proof/`, it measures the last good commit's own test selection and dev's head side by side on one fixed real change. It raises an alarm at 5 times as long, past the 360-file cap, a run that fails, or a 600 s backstop. On an alarm it tells you with a Windows notification, turns the express window off, holds those folders against commits, and, when exactly one landing changed them and its revert applies cleanly, puts that revert on your card. It lifts its holds when the measurement is good again. Replayed on the real case: b52ac43e (before e51b2c7f) took 28.5 s, e51b2c7f was still running at 300 s, and b6fdcf85 took 39.9 s. Register it with `scripts/pipeline_canary/enable_pipeline_canary.ps1`; see `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-pipeline-canary-design.md`.
