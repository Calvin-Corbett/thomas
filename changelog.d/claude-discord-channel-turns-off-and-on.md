### Changed
- **The Discord channel can be turned off and on while Thomas runs.** Turning it off takes away the Channels page's ten routes and stops the Discord bridge, so the bot goes offline and nothing it hears reaches Thomas; your bot token and settings stay, and turning it back on starts the bridge again only if it was running when you turned it off. Closing Thomas now stops the bridge the same way and starting Thomas brings it back. The helper on the Channels page reads and changes nothing while this is off, where before it could build a bridge runtime of its own, and with it off the Channels page asks once and waits instead of asking about 20 times a second.
