### Changed
- Your landing window can now stay open until you turn it off: `window_control.py open --until-off` asks for the longest window, a year at most (it was 24 hours), and the card you sign names the date it would end. You chose this on 2026-10-27: "Until I turn it off". It still ends when you close it, switch windows off, sign out of Windows, or the promoter restarts.
- A change whose only edit to `pyproject.toml` or `thomas/__init__.py` is the version line, raised to the next patch number, can now land in a window. Every change to Thomas's code bumps that line, and `pyproject.toml` is on the enforcement list, so before this no change to Thomas's code could land without a card. A version jump or a step back still comes to your card, as does every other guard, cage or network change.
