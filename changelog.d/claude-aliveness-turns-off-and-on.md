### Changed

- **The idle awareness sweep can now be turned off and on from Settings, under Plugins, while Thomas runs.** Every five minutes or so it looks at Thomas's own surroundings and writes down what it would have said, without ever saying it. It begins off, as it always did. If you start Thomas with its aliveness setting on and have never chosen on the Plugins page, it begins on and remembers that, and after that what you choose there wins over the setting. The command line still starts it from the setting alone. Turning it off waits for a look that is partway through, then ends the sweep. Stop now ends only that wait, and the look finishes by itself. Turning it off and on again quickly can no longer leave the old sweep running beside the new one when a look takes longer than two seconds.
