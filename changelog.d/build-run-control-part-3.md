### Added

- Build run control, part 3 of 3: a Build run now gets a control folder, so you can pause it, resume it, message the lead or an agent, and answer its questions; a pause holds the lead, its helpers and its tool waits, and gives the held time back to their time limits. A pass that ends early becomes a decision (continue, ask you, or stop), and the launch and status replies say what the run's lead can take.
