### Changed

- **Your saved interface changes can now be turned off and on while Thomas runs.** The three `/api/ui/overlay` routes and the step that puts your saved changes into every page moved out of the server's fixed list into a row called Saved interface changes, which ships on, so nothing changes until you turn it off. While it is off, every page you open comes up as Thomas ships it, and nothing is deleted: the file of saved changes is not touched, and when you turn it back on the pages you open next show every change exactly as before. Opening Settings, Mission Control, Library or Virtual Office while it is off makes that browser forget which of your saved themes you had chosen, so you choose it again once the row is back on. A page already open keeps its look until you reload it. Redesign with AI still opens with the row off, but a change to a colour or a name is not applied and a change to a part of a page shows only in that browser, and its note says to reload and Apply again, which does not help. A save under way when you turn the row off finishes first. System Map, Agent Ops and Library never showed your saved changes, and still do not.
