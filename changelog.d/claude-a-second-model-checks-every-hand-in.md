### Added

- **A second model reads every change Thomas makes to himself before it reaches your card.** Right after the check before the card passes, Claude, on your Claude plan and able only to read, gets the request, the change and what the check found, and reads the change in the team's own copy. The teams build with GPT, so a different model is checking another's work. SAFE: the change goes to your card, and the card says a second model read it. NOT SAFE: it goes back to its team with the findings as a repair round, the way a failed check does, and never reaches you. When the reviewer cannot run (your plan's usage limit, a sign-in that ended), the change is held rather than sent to you unchecked, and it is tried again every 15 minutes for a day without running its tests again while the change has not moved. Until now this check was done by hand in another session, and twice a change it had found not safe reached your card anyway. `THOMAS_SECOND_CHECK=off` turns it off.
