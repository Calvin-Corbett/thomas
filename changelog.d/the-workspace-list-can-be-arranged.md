### Added

- **You can drag, rename and remove the Workspaces in the sidebar.** You asked for "click and drag a workspace and organize it however you want" and to "right click on workspaces to remove them. Or to rename them", and the list was seven fixed buttons that did none of it. Drag a row to move it (a line shows where it will land; the list scrolls while you drag near its edge). Right-click a row to rename it in place, move it up or down, or remove it; `Alt`+arrow keys move the focused row and `F2` renames it. Removing hides a workspace and does not uninstall it: a `N hidden` row appears under the list and brings any of them back, or resets everything. Your order, names and removals are saved in this browser. A workspace you have not arranged yet, such as a newly installed plugin, shows up after the ones you placed, so it cannot go missing.

### Fixed

- **The Workspaces list now keeps its own scroll.** It could be taller than the room it had and give you no reliable way to reach the rows below the edge. It scrolls inside its own box and keeps the mouse wheel to itself.
