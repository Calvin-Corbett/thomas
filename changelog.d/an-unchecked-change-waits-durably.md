### Fixed

- **A change that could not be checked waits.** When the check before your approval card cannot run (too little free disk, no interpreter, a scratch copy of dev that cannot be made, its one-hour deadline, tests that cannot run on dev itself), the finished change is now held before your card instead of reaching it marked "Not checked". For a change Autopilot hands in, Thomas tries the check again by himself every 15 minutes, up to 96 times. A change handed in again after a refusal, and a ratchet repair, are held the same way but not on that clock: the hand-in again stops and records why, and the ratchet keeps the work for its next run.
- **A hold survives a restart.** The hold, like one whose second look could not run, is now kept on disk instead of in memory, so a restart no longer forgets it; a check a restart interrupted is tried again a minute later, and the chat's card follows it again.
- **Your Stop and your steering come first.** A held or running check is never handed in after you stop or steer the project, or once a newer run has written to its copy; a steer you send to a held change starts its run at once.
- **Plain words while it waits.** The project list and the chat's card say what the change is waiting for and when it is tried next. When its tries run out, or the check fails to run three times for a reason other than the disk, it stops with one recorded issue and says "Never handed in" instead of waiting for ever.
- **An empty change stays empty.** An empty change is handed off as empty instead of being read again, and a card whose second look was turned off now says so.
