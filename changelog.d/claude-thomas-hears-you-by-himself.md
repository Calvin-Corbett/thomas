### Added

- **Thomas now hears you live by himself, with nothing to click and no other program open.** Live talk used to need Voice Studio running and a licence accepted in its Model Catalogue. That click was Voice Studio's own rule for every model it has not marked commercial; the model it wanted, NVIDIA's Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3, is free to use under CC BY 4.0, which asks only for credit. Thomas now runs that model himself, on this computer's processor, with the open-source sherpa-onnx engine: it loads once in about 4 seconds, then writes down a 4-second sentence in under half a second, with punctuation, in 25 European languages. He knows when you have finished from your pause. Voice Studio still gives him your Miles voice when it is open; when Thomas's speech model is not installed he listens with Voice Studio or your browser, as before. Thomas keeps the model in his own install folder as well as beside his settings, because the caged server's settings folder is in its own Windows account, where nothing else can put it. Turning live talk on no longer moves the message box: the line that shows what Thomas hears takes the place of the note under the box. That line names your voice ("myles") instead of its code, because Thomas now keeps Voice Studio's voice list, which takes it about 2 seconds to send.
