### Added
- The self-project park tick proposes up to two projects per hourly scan from pytest's `.pytest_cache/v/cache/lastfailed` and recent JUnit reports in `.thomas/evolve/planner/reports/`, issue ledger entries, and Chat pipeline request and response-timing events. Each queued project records its evidence origin and source ID; duplicate subjects (including quoted existing work) and a three-project pending cap limit the backlog.
- Chat evidence scanning keeps the latest 2,000 events per recent log file; requests explicitly targeting improvements to Thomas remain eligible even when Chat already responded. Requests to plan Thomas's own improvement work are eligible, and equivalent imperative, “Can you improve Thomas search?”, “Thomas, can you fix your search?”, and “Please improve search in Thomas” requests share one project subject rather than duplicating work.
- The Improve Thomas dialog displays queued improvements with their stored `origin` and source ID, and offers a switch to pause automatic planning and starts.
- `GET` and `PUT /api/self/projects/queue/planner` expose a persistent `enabled` switch. Setting it to false pauses automated proposals and the start of previously queued automated projects without cancelling them; manual queue entries remain eligible. `THOMAS_SELF_IMPROVEMENT_PLANNER=off` also disables planning and automated starts.
