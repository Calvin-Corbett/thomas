### Changed

- **The deck's tiles are live miniatures, and a click shows that output in the middle of the chat.** You said the top-bar tile should not be a little console square. A game or page is now a small running copy of itself, a picture is the picture, and a PDF keeps an icon. Hover a tile and its kind and name appear under it ("Web page · index.html"). Click it and that one output opens large in the middle of the chat, over a dimmed background: not full screen and not the side panel. It has a new-tab link and an X; Escape and a click outside close it too. Click into a game to play it.
- **The empty-Build slideshow is bigger, calmer and nicer to watch.** The arrows are wide chevrons with no circle around them, the dots are faint with a longer one for the slide you are on, and everything is larger (titles, numbers, bars). A slide settles in from the right while the old one softens away, then its title, its figures and its bars arrive one after another; the big numbers count up and the bars grow.
