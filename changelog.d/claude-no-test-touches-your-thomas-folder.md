- **Test runs leave your `~/.thomas` alone.** After your data folder was closed to tests, a 54-file sample still made 597 writes to `C:\Users\<you>\.thomas`. Your Build projects, chat workspaces and code scratch are found from `THOMAS_OWNED_ROOT` or your home folder, not from the data folder. `test_chat_delegation_worker_contract` had made 23 "Use browseropen and browserextract then …" projects in your Build list, 13 of them on 2026-09-24. Engine tests appended to your `~/.thomas/*_engine.jsonl`, and `test_distribution` wrote test values over your updater's `state.json`. The test run now points `THOMAS_OWNED_ROOT` at its own temp folder before anything imports Thomas, and drops inherited `THOMAS_*` paths into your `~/.thomas`. It also moves the folder that the five engines and the updater fix at import time, until those have an override of their own. If a top-level file in your `~/.thomas` or any `~/.thomas/projects/*` folder changes while the tests run, the run fails and names it, as your data files already did. Nothing in your `~/.thomas` was deleted, so the 23 test-made projects are still there for you to remove if you want. Two things can still reach your `~/.thomas` from a test, because nothing can redirect them yet. One is the self-edit lock and candidate folders. The other is the project list that a test-started server reads from this checkout, which names your real projects.
