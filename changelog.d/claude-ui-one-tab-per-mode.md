### Changed

- **Chat, Build and Work each keep one tab.** Clicking a conversation or a build in the sidebar now opens it in the tab you are in, instead of adding a tab for every one. A conversation gets a tab of its own only when you ask: right-click it and choose "Open in its own tab", or Ctrl-click or middle-click it. One case still opens a tab without asking: if the tab you are in has a turn still running, or words typed and not sent, the row opens beside it so neither is lost.
- **Search is an icon beside New chat.** Pressing it turns the whole row into the search box. Escape, or leaving the box empty, gives New chat back.
- **Build and Work have new icons.** Build is a pair of code brackets and Work is a tick, drawn as lines to match the Chat icon. The hammer and the briefcase are gone.
