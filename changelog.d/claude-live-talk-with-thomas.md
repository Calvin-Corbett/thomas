### Changed

- **Talking to Thomas is now a live conversation, and the microphone itself is the switch.** You said voice mode was not working, that it felt "turn-based", and that it should work "where you talk to it and talks right back to you." Rest the pointer on the microphone under the message box: a ring fills around it, and after a moment it stretches into a slider with a speaker at the far end. Click it and the knob slides across, the microphone becomes a speaker, and live talk is on. Speak, pause, and what you said is sent at once; Thomas reads his answer aloud a sentence at a time while he is still writing it, and he stops as soon as you start talking over him. Click the switch again to turn it off. A quick click on the microphone still types what you say, as before. The sound-wave button and the small arrow beside it are gone; right-click the switch, or click the voice's name under the message box, to change the voice.
- **Spoken questions no longer go to a picture model.** The rule that picks your smallest and fastest model chose `google/nano-banana-2-lite` because its name carries "nano", and that model makes pictures, not answers. The model list also marked every DeepInfra model as a chat model, including picture, video, speech and embedding models. Those are no longer called chat models, and the voice only picks a model that answers in words.
- **Live listening uses Voice Studio's fast processor models.** Thomas now hears you through Voice Studio's live listening line instead of sending one recording at the end, which took 35 seconds or more on this computer because the large Whisper model had to run on the processor while the graphics card was full. Voice Studio's live models each need their licence accepted once in its Model Catalogue; until one is, Thomas listens with the browser's own hearing and says why.
