### Changed
- While the hard CPU cap holds Thomas under 90%, his work no longer waits for the PC's CPU to drop. In its first hours the step log showed each team test run waiting about 90 seconds for room (30 minutes over 20 runs in 2.4 hours) because the PC was at or over 90% most of the time, much of it from other programs such as Windows Defender, Windows Search and your own apps. With the cap set, that wait only made Thomas give way and then run anyway. Work still waits while memory is over the limit, and every wait comes back if the cap could not be set.
- The step log compresses each past day (about eight times smaller). Ten teams write roughly 40 MB of steps a day, which over 90 days would have filled several gigabytes. Packed days are still read by `/api/metrics/steps`.
