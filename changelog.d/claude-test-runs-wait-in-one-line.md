### Changed

- **Test runs wait their turn in one line for the whole PC instead of all starting at once.** You asked to "scale up to as many as possible under 90% cpu". On 2026-11-13 ten Builds ran thirteen test runs at once on a PC already at 90%: 48 were stopped at the 9-minute limit with nothing learned (7.2 hours of them), a check before a card ran 92 minutes and ended "unavailable", and nothing landed. A test run a Build asks for now takes a ticket and starts when its turn comes, first come first served. No more start at once than the 90% limit leaves processor threads for, and while the PC is at its limit only two run at a time. The wait is no longer taken out of the 9 minutes a run has, and nothing cuts it off: when the tests have no verdict after 8 minutes the tool answers "queued" or "running", and the same call again goes on waiting for the same run. The checks before a card are not in this line yet.
