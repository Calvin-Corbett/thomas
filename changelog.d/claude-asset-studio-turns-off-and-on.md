### Changed

- **Asset Studio and ComfyUI can now be turned off and on while Thomas runs.** The 39 `/api/asset-studio/v1` routes moved out of the server's fixed list into a row, which ships on, so nothing changes until you turn it off. While it is off those routes answer 404, and nothing you normally use changes, because no button in the sidebar or Settings opens the Asset Studio page and no page shows these jobs. A job still running is ended and marked cancelled, a program set to hear when jobs finish is told, and your jobs, templates and that setting come back when you turn it on. A ComfyUI that Thomas started can keep running, and after that Thomas cannot stop it, so the row's words tell you to close it yourself. A page left open keeps what it last showed until you press something, and a reloaded one says Comfy is offline even if ComfyUI is running. A server started with the row off never loads the routes' code. Turned off on a running server, the routes go at once and their code stays loaded until the next restart.
