### Fixed

- Project listings and queue recovery skip individual denied, malformed or oversized conversation records and denied project directories with a warning naming the project. Conversation readers stream bounded metadata and turn summaries instead of retaining full conversation payloads; records over 2 MB and summaries over 64 KiB are skipped.
- Project preparation returns a plain refusal after eight seconds rather than leaving the start request waiting indefinitely. Regression coverage includes blocked registration and verifies that a timed-out start cannot submit later.
- Structurally malformed metadata, retirement, repair and turn fields are skipped with a project-naming warning. Legacy “start could not be confirmed” notices retry after the recovery delay without retaining that stale reason.
- Could not start notices show their recorded time and clear for registered projects. Interrupted-start notices retry after the recovery delay. Exhausted disk-space failures rearm after their retry delay and a fresh free-space check succeeds. Permission failures rearm after the named path permits read/write access. Exhausted preparation-timeout and busy starts admit one delayed recovery attempt at a time, doubling the delay up to one hour after repeated refusals without granting a fresh retry batch. A later failure discards an earlier permission blocker. Timeout and connection exceptions and temporary OS resource failures (including unavailable resources, memory pressure, and exhausted file descriptors) persist a transient blocker and use the same delayed single-attempt recovery. Permanent OS and validation refusals retain their retry limit. Negative interrupted-start lookups refresh after the recovery grace period so a late registration is found before retrying. Queued retry notices also show their failure time.
